          Simplify geometry
          
    
          
      Rebuilds chains of imported stroke polygons into centerline
      transmission lines and thins densely sampled curves. Copper edges
      move by at most the tolerance; the mesh then follows one clean pair
      of edges instead of hundreds of overlapping outlines.
